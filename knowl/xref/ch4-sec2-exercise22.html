(a)

Show that the position \(x(t)\) of a body that falls against air resistance from an initial height of \(x_0\) meters is given by the formula
\begin{equation*} x(t) = x_0 - \dfrac{g}{b} \, t - \dfrac{g}{b^2} \left(2^{-b\,t/0.69} - 1\right) \ \mbox{meters}. \end{equation*}
